# Reference used for grading, reviewed November 6, 2026

These are manual fact cards from the public pages, independent of the app's catalog, ranking and response templates. Review the pages before changing the oracle. The suite is offline and cannot certify future website edits.

* [School workshops (DE)](https://forju.at/de/projects/schulworkshops) / [EN](https://forju.at/en/projects/schulworkshops). The article describes workshops for Viennese schools, including conflict resolution, environment and media literacy (Instagram, TikTok and fake news). Wiener Bildungschancen funds 100% of seminars for school years 1–9. It directs interested visitors to arrange an appointment with Anja Hofer at anja.hofer@forju.at. It does not publish a registration deadline, guaranteed slot or per-child price outside Vienna.
* [Resources (DE)](https://forju.at/de/services/ressourcen) / [EN](https://forju.at/en/services/ressourcen). Partners provide access to rooms and sharing programmes for measuring instruments and specialist equipment. ForJu handles organisation, including liability and supervision. Requests are possible. The article is not a live inventory and promises neither a particular instrument nor a date.
* [Financial support (DE)](https://forju.at/de/services/finanziell) / [EN](https://forju.at/en/services/finanziell). ForJu does not sponsor projects directly. It helps with estimates and feasibility and makes introductions to funding partners. There is no blanket eligibility promise, published personal award amount or universal application deadline.
* [Donations (DE)](https://forju.at/de/spenden) describes intended expenditure including project materials and travel to competitions. Campaign plans do not establish entitlement to reimbursement. [The partnership contact (EN)](https://forju.at/en/kontakt?intent=partner) names Alexander Bayer and gives his ForJu email for institutional cooperation. Nothing confirms that an offer has been accepted.
* Clarification has no supporting page until the visitor identifies an offer. Suggestions are useful and a citation to an arbitrarily selected programme would be misleading.

Each case lists the required fact, exact language-specific supporting page, unknowns and forbidden claims. Grade the final turn with deterministic checks and retain all intermediate replies for manual assessment of qualifications, tone, incidental claims and citation entailment. A matching URL alone is insufficient.
