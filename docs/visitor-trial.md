# ForJu Compass visitor trial

Open the [German website preview](https://forju-compass.onrender.com/preview) or the [English preview](https://forju-compass.onrender.com/preview?lang=en). Both load the released widget on a Compass-hosted preview page. Test participants can open it without an account. Allow about five minutes per person.

**Adam’s part.** Invite a teacher, a student and a ForJu team member. Give each person one goal below. Ask them to use their own words, read the linked ForJu page and tell you what they would do next. Use fictional details. Stop at the editable draft or official form, before sending anything.

| Visitor | Task to give them | What to check afterwards |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Teacher | „Deine Klasse in Graz möchte einen Klimaworkshop. Finde heraus, ob das möglich ist und was es pro Kind kostet. Bereite eine Anfrage vor.“ | Compass distinguishes the published Vienna offer from a visit to Graz. The price needs confirmation. The draft goes to the workshop contact listed on the linked article. |
| Student | “Your class is studying air quality. You want to speak with someone who researches it. Find a way to ask for an introduction.” | The visitor reaches the introductions form or an editable enquiry. Offer them a chance to change a detail in the draft. |
| ForJu team member | „Du brauchst für eine frühere Spende eine Bestätigung. Finde heraus, an wen du schreiben und welche Angaben du machen sollst.“ | The expected destination is the donation page. A draft should address office@forju.at and ask for the date and amount. |

Have at least one person use iPhone Safari. With the keyboard open, check they can see what they type and send it. Follow a source, return, close the chat and reopen it within the same page. Ask whether they can find their conversation again.

Record four things per task: whether the visitor finished unaided, whether the linked page supports the answer, whether the visitor knew the next step, and the first point of confusion (if any). Share the task, the step and a paraphrase of the problem. There is no need to share names, chat logs, or real donation details.

If the testers agree on the answers and can finish unaided, the authorized website editor can use the [existing installation instructions](embed.md). Ask the workshop team for a publishable source if they can confirm current pricing or coverage beyond Vienna.
